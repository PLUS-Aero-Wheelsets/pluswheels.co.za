G5 Silk Ceramic Hybrid Bearings

G5 Silk bearings are ceramic hybrid units designed for hubs, bottom brackets and pulley systems. Si3N4 silicon nitride balls (Grade 5) run on stainless steel outer races with a PEEK self-lubricating cage. Thin wall seals, ABEC 7 precision rating, C3 internal clearance, and Kluber Isoflex lubrication. The result: lower rolling resistance, longer service life and better corrosion resistance than any standard steel bearing.

Balls: Si3N4 Silicon Nitride (Grade 5)  |  Cage: PEEK Self-Lubricating
Outer Race: Stainless Steel  |  Seals: Thin Wall
ABEC Rating: 7  |  Clearance: C3
Lubrication: Kluber Isoflex NBA 52
Use: Hubs - BB - Pulleys  |  Series: G5 Silk

Interlock Bottom Brackets

The Interlock BB is built for performance, not for leisure riding. CNC machined from 7075-T6 aluminium with no rubber dust seals - the bearing seats directly into the housing for maximum contact and zero play. Guaranteed to deliver frictionless, effective drivetrain efficiency from the very point where your cranks meet the frame. Warranty covers bearings only.
