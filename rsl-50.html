RACE FAST. ALL DAY.

The RSL 50 is the wheelset you race, not just train on. Toroidal rim profile, Alpina Extra Lite spokes, RS240 hub standard. Available in white or black branding.

TOROIDAL RIM PROFILE

The 50mm toroidal profile balances aerodynamic efficiency with crosswind stability. At 31mm outer width it pairs optimally with 25–30mm tyres for maximum contact patch and comfort.

RS240 HUB — UPGRADABLE

RS240 runs as standard. Upgrade to the RS180 for weight savings, or add the 60T conical ratchet for 6-degree engagement. Both available at +R1,000 — choose one.
